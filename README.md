# ❄️ FreezeX

### Lock the Rate. Move the Money.

**FreezeX** is a blockchain-enabled cross-border payment and freelance marketplace prototype designed to make international payments between clients and freelancers more transparent, predictable, and automated.

The platform connects **Clients**, **Freelancers**, and an **Admin** through a unified marketplace and payment-orchestration system.

---

## 🚀 Problem

Cross-border payments for freelancers can involve:

- High transaction fees
- Hidden FX markups
- Unpredictable exchange rates
- Delayed settlements
- Manual payment processing
- Identity and compliance risks
- Fraud risks
- Lack of transparency between project completion and payment

For example, a US company hiring an Indian freelancer may agree on a fixed USD amount, but the freelancer may receive a different INR value because of exchange-rate changes and additional charges.

FreezeX addresses this problem by introducing **transparent FX rate locking and automatic payment orchestration**.
